Common questions

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

The app can't read my NFC tag.
Hold the top edge of your iPhone (or the middle of the back of an Android phone) flat on the tag for a second. Phone cases with metal or magnets can block NFC.
