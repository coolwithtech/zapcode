Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Zapcode: Scan, Create & Tap app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app on iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

  • There is no account. Your scans, the codes you create, your history and your settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The camera, photos, contacts and NFC are used on your device only, when you use a feature that needs them.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. Ad partners may use device identifiers to show and measure ads, according to your choices.
  • We use Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, so we can fix bugs and improve the app.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Data that stays on your device

WhatWhy
CameraTo scan QR codes and barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere.
PhotosOnly when you choose a picture to scan, or save a QR code to your photo library.
ContactsOnly when you pick a contact to turn into a QR code or NFC tag. Only the contact you pick is read.
NFCTo read and write NFC tags you hold near your phone.
History, created codes, settingsSaved on your device so you can find them again. Deleting the app or clearing history removes them.

Scanned content (for example a website address) is only opened when you tap Open, or automatically if you turn on "Open links right away". The website you open has its own privacy policy.

Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Firebase privacy.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iPhone): the app asks for permission before ads may use your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): Google's consent form is shown before personalized ads. You can change your choice in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • No ads: Pro subscribers don't see ads and no ad requests are made for them.
  • Permissions: camera, photos, contacts and NFC can be turned off at any time in your phone's settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we don't have accounts and keep your content on your device, most of your data is already under your control. For data held by Google, you can use the choices above or contact us and we will help.

The legal bases we rely on are your consent (personalized ads, where required), and our legitimate interests in keeping the app working and secure (crash reports, anonymous statistics).

Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it or the app. Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.
